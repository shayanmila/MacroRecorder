MASOOM MACRO RECORDER - PRIVACY INFORMATION
Last updated: 7 November 2026

Masoom Macro Recorder is a Windows application that records keyboard and mouse activity at your request.

WHAT IS RECORDED
When recording is enabled, the application may capture keystrokes, pointer movement, mouse clicks, and timing. This can include sensitive information entered while recording.

WHERE DATA IS STORED
Recorded events are held in application memory. When you choose Save, they are written to a JSON file at the location you select. Diagnostic logs may be written under your Windows LocalAppData folder if an error occurs.

NETWORK AND THIRD PARTIES
The current application source does not implement an account, cloud synchronization, or an upload of recorded events. This statement describes the current application code; it does not cover Windows, .NET, or third-party software running on your computer.

YOUR CHOICES
You control when recording starts and stops, which input categories to record, and whether to save or delete your local files. Avoid recording passwords, payment details, or private conversations.
